LEMMIQ - PLAY PROTECT / MANUAL TESTING
======================================

There is no legitimate APK/file that bypasses Google Play Protect.

Before you have a Google Play Console account, use either:

1. PWA TESTING - recommended
   https://lemmiq-api.onrender.com/web/
   Install from the browser to the Home Screen.

2. MANUAL RELEASE-SIGNED APK
   Keep your existing LEMMIQ upload-keystore.jks and keystore.properties.
   Run:

   cd C:\Users\pc\LEMMIQ\android
   build-manual-test-apk.cmd

   Output:
   app\build\outputs\manual\LEMMIQ-v2.8.2-manual-release.apk

This release APK is signed with YOUR existing LEMMIQ upload key. It is better
for continuity than distributing random debug APKs, but Play Protect can still
show a scan/unknown-source warning because the install is outside Google Play.

Do not ask testers to disable Play Protect.

When you later create a Play Console account:
- enable Play App Signing;
- upload the AAB to Internal Testing;
- invite tester Google accounts;
- set Render ANDROID_PLAY_URL to the Internal Testing link;
- remove/blank ANDROID_APK_URL if you no longer want sideload distribution.
